
# Adventure Background
- **Archangel Avacyn** is trapped within the *Helvault*, after a desperate struggle with the demon **Griselbrand**. Saving her, would mean shattering the *Helvault* and releasing hundreds of years worth of captured demons.
- A mystical tuning fork, an artifact in church's possession is the key to free **Avacyn** without releasing the demons. 
- The elders have secretly delivered the artifact to a musical prodigy in *Stensia*, named **Katarina Brunn**, so she can use her expertise to unlock the artifacts power.
- **Katarina** is ordered to study the artifacts powers in her isolated home town of *Shadowgrange*, far away from the influence of *Skirsdag*, a secret demon-worshiping cult.

---

- **Katarina** makes some progress in uncovering the secrets of the artifact.
- **Katarina** and **Siegfried** tried to rally the villagers and defend themselves from the vampires, but the village gave up after **Avacyn's** disappearance.
- 2 months ago, vampires from *Volden Manor* attacked to claim **Katarina** for their dark festival, the *Cour of the Vampire Queen*. Katarina, Siegfried and **Ralph** fought the vampires, but with the village refusing to help, Ralph was killed, Katarina captured and Siegfried forced to flee with the artifact.

---

- **Siegfried** swore revenge on Vampires and *Shadowgrange*, took refuge in an abandoned *Maurer Estate* and turned to the dark necro-alchemy, to create *skaabs*.
- **Siegfried** would need more bodies for creating *skaabs* and defeating the vampires, so he allied with **Rinelda Smit** with intention to attack *Shadowgrange*, forcing the villagers to fight the vampires in death, since they refused to do so in life.

# People & Places
- *Shadowgrange* - an isolated village deep in *Stensia*, surrounded by **vampire estates**. Its people are proud and traditional on the outside, but they hide deep fear. Through generations of vampire attacks, they learned to accept them as inevitable.
- *Volden Manor* - a vampire manor

---

- **Katarina** was once a paladin and a member of the *Riders of Gavony* and therefore became the leader of the *Shadowgrange miilitia*. She was chosen by the elders of the Church to uncover the secret power within the mystical tuning fork, that is the key to freeing Avacyn and saving Innistrad.
- **Siegfried** is an alchemist and Katarina's fionce
- **Ralph Brunn** was a mayor of Shadowgrange and an ally of Katrina and Siegfried
- **Rinelda Smit** is a ghoulcaller, helping Siegfried in creating skaabs.
- *Skaab* is a malformed "offspring" created by skaberen, often using corpse parts.

## Villagers
Most of villagers are **farmers** and most farmers are **shepherds**. The rest are supporting the farmers through jobs, such as *spinning wool, weaving clothes, shoeing horses, milling grain*. But shipment jobs move less frequently with roads becoming more dangerous. There are **more and more villagers**, that immigrate from nearby villages, that get destroyed in the monster attacks. Those are becoming **more frequent and bold**, due to weakening of Church of Avacyn's power. Many villagers **believe the village will fall**. The villagers are **under stress** due to the need to accommodate and feed their growing population. The villagers resent Thraben, believing the seat of church isn't doing enough to help villages like Shadowgrange.

The people in Shadowgrange are **stoic** and **guard their hearts**, since the frequent vampire attacks robbed many of their relatives and loved ones. They are **firm in their beliefs**, but **appear cold** to outsiders because of them. Initially they are **wary of the players**, but they can earn the villagers respect by proving their ability to defend the village.
## Village
The walls around the village, make it the best defended village in the area. The farms are outside the village walls. Every cottage is built around a *hawthorn tree*, which provides *[[armyofthedamned.pdf#page=63|living wood]]* as a defense against the vampires.

### Bountiful Heron General Store
Bountiful Heron is the biggest trading post in Shadowgrange. Players can buy gear, with the exception of items worth over 25 gp. If players desire metal weapons or armor, they are pointed towards [[#The Tempered Bull]] and if they need ranged weapons or leather gear they are told about the [[#Fleeting Fox Provisions]].

>[!npc] Otto & Magda Botler

#### Saint Traft's Memorial
Flora is happy to show adventurers the memorial. Traft was a young priest from Stensia. He specialized in fighting demons, with even warrior-seraphs fighting alongside him and trusting his battle skills. He became recognized as a saint before his fortieth year. His repeated slaying of the demons frustrated their plans. They plotted revenge at Needle's Eye, where Traft was tricked into killing several people and murdered by the demon-lord **Withengar**. His spirit still manifests through Stensia.

If the players chose to visit his memorial, [[armyofthedamned.pdf#page=20|describe]] the room.
The display case is locked, but a DC 20 Sleight of Hand (Dexterity) check can open it. Flora, however, doesn't let anyone touch the case, since inside is supposed to be *[[armyofthedamned.pdf#page=64|Elbrus, the Binding Blade]]*, but it was stolen by [[#^ottomagda|Otto and Magda Botler]] and replaced with a convincing duplicate. A magic spell *Detect Magic* or something similar, can detect the duplicate. 

>[!npc] Traft's Spirit
>**Quest: Saint Traft's Favor** At some point later in the adventure of DMs choosing, Traft appears in the dream of an adventurer, which pays respect at his shrine, wields a spear or acts in a lawful or good manner. Read [[armyofthedamned.pdf#page=20|this]], when that time comes. Upon waking up, the adventurer finds the *[[armyofthedamned.pdf#page=65|Moonsivler Spear]]* in their hands.
### Alchemist's Shop
Before entering the shop, describe it's exterior:
>[!scroll] Alchemy Shop Exterior
>*Hanging above the front door of this small cabin is a wooden sign with a potion painted on it. Like most of the buildings here, a hawthorn tree pokes out of the cabin’s roof in the center, but its barren branches and greying bark signals that it’s dying.*

The shop has been abandoned, since Siegfried fled from Shadowgrange after Katarina's kidnapping. The shelves are stocked with about 350 gp worht of alchemical materials that can be used for [[Player's Handbook.pdf#page=188|crafting]]. On the counter are the following items, *10 empty vials and stoppers, herbalism kit and alchemist's supplies*, see [[Player's Handbook.pdf#page=155|Tools]]. Hanging on the back wall are a couple of expired potions, along with *4 vials of acid, 3 potions of healing and 2 alchemist's fire*, see [[Player's Handbook.pdf#page=151|Adventuring Gear]]. Hanging over the fireplace are various iron mixing pots. Inside one of them is a muddy liquid, that has little bits of scales, pink flesh, a small claw and hair. A DC 15 Nature (Inteligence) check reveals it to be a potion of *[[2024 Dungeon_Masters_Guide - Wizards_of_the_Coast.pdf#page=291|animal friendship]]*. A DC 12 Investigation (Inteligence) check notices badly concealed ashen footprints leading from the fireplace and out the door. An additional DC 15 Investigation (Inteligence) check of the fireplace notices a hidden lever, that upon being pulled, reveals a narrow spiraling staircase that leads to the Alchemist's Basement.

The basement is pitch black, so the characters will need dark vision or a source of light to see.
>[!scroll] Alchemist's Basement

>
>*Along the walls on both your sides are strange alchemical devices, glass bottles of various shapes and sizes filled with strange liquids, tubes connecting them with each other and others burying themselves amid the tree’s roots.*
>
>*Beside the back wall is a wooden desk with piles of papers covering its surface along with a leather-bound book. Beside the desk is a humanoid figure laying down on a metal platform with dozens of utensils littering its sides.*

In the basement, there are [[Monster Manual.pdf#page=33|5 twig blights and 1 vine blight]], a Siegfried attempt at creating a living weapon against the vampires. These plants have devoured most of the nutrients from the hawthorn tree and now wait for the opportunity to attack the party.
